Management validation settings. Nothing here is hard-coded permanently. Change a rule, press Apply Settings, and compare the recommendations with experienced purchasing decisions.

Target stock cover

Months of demand to reach when a reorder is triggered.

Safety stock

Extra buffer months added to the target.

Critical cover

Projected cover below this becomes Critical Order.

Reorder-soon cover

Projected cover below this triggers replenishment toward target.

Overstock cover

Current physical stock above this cover is flagged Overstock.

Dormant inactivity

Months without a sale before stock is held for manual review.

Dead stock inactivity

Months of inactivity while stock remains. Never-sold items use Last Purchase Date when available, otherwise consecutive zero-sales months.

Fast moving threshold

Smart demand rate in pieces/month.

Medium moving threshold

Below Fast and at/above this rate = Medium.

One-time spike dominance

If one month contributes this percentage of sales with very few active months, automatic purchase is blocked for review.

Regular-demand active months

Percentage of elapsed months that should contain sales before demand is treated as regular.

Other-brand stock credit

Percentage of exact-same-OEM other-brand stock/incoming deducted. Default 0% until management approves.

Use brand lead time

When enabled, the planning horizon considers each brand's lead time plus safety cover.

Default lead time

Fallback delivery lead time in days. Override individual brands in the table below.

Apply MOQ / order multiple

Optional because real MOQ can be product-specific. Purchaser can still manually edit Suggested Qty.

Default MOQ

Fallback minimum order quantity. Keep 0 when not applicable.

Default order multiple

Rounds an order upward to this multiple. Keep 1 for no rounding.

Demand calculation

Smart mode protects against old sales spikes and reacts to current trends.

Suggestion mode

Proactive mode matches the management-planning use case. Min/Max behaves more like a traditional replenishment trigger.

Brand Purchasing Rules
Set lead time per brand. MOQ and order multiple are optional; leave them at 0 / 1 when not applicable.
SHJ Replenishment Settings
Temporary stock-balancing assumptions until actual Sharjah monthly sales are available in Odoo.

Estimated SHJ share

Temporary percentage of overall smart demand assumed to belong to Sharjah.

Normal SHJ cover

Target weeks kept locally for non-fast movers. Weekly transfers mean this can stay low.

Fast-mover SHJ cover

Extra local cover for products moving quickly overall.

Motorline reserve

Estimated months of Motorline demand that must remain before stock is transferred out.

Bavaria as nearby backup

Bavaria is close to Sharjah, so its stock can cover the local requirement before requesting Motorline transfer.

Minimum group demand

Do not recommend SHJ stocking below this overall smart-demand rate; low-demand items stay for review.
